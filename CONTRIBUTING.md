# Community and contribution policy

## Read this before opening an issue

This repository is a public information hub during private beta. It does not accept requests for private source, unreleased builds, extracted files, test worlds, assets, or access to TROAINC production systems.

Useful reports are specific, reproducible, and respectful. Include:

1. Space Engineers version and whether the session is single player, hosted multiplayer, or dedicated server.
2. A short description of what happened and what was expected.
3. Exact Aegis hardware on the affected construct and whether it is a ship or station.
4. Relevant Console status text, steps to reproduce, and unedited logs when an authorized test build is available.
5. Compatibility context, especially WeaponCore and other combat mods.

## Not accepted

- Demands for release dates, private code, or unreleased packages.
- Copied third-party source or assets.
- Copyrighted franchise files, sounds, models, logos, fonts, ships, or UI materials.
- Harassment, personal attacks, threats, dogpiling, spam, or attempts to pressure maintainers.
- Reuploads, leaks, reverse engineering of unreleased files, or requests to bypass access restrictions.

## Expectations

TROAINC welcomes clear feedback and reports in good faith. Maintainership decisions, release timing, scope, licensing, and moderation remain with TROAINC. A report being read does not guarantee a reply, implementation, early access, or release date.

## Security and compatibility concerns

Use TROAINC’s authorized private contact channels for security, exploit, multiplayer corruption, or private-build concerns. Do not publish exploit instructions or private material in public issues.
